## RELATIONAL OPERATORS
relational operators are operators that let you compare a value to another
there are 6 relational

**the greater than(>):** this simply means a value is bigger than the other
**the less than(<):** this means a value is less than the other
**the greater than or equal >=:** this means a value is bigger or equal to other
**the less than or equal <=:** this means a value is less than or equal to other
**the equal (==):** this means a value is 100% equal to the other
**the inequality (!=):** this means a value is not equal to other
